SERVE TEAMS

Departments You Can Join

There are many ways to serve at Streams of Joy South London. Choose the area that fits your gifts and passion.

Choir

Singers, musicians, and band members who lead worship.

Ushering

Welcoming people and helping create an excellent church experience.

Protocol

Supporting leadership and ensuring services run smoothly.

Media

Camera operation, livestream, projection, and sound.

Social Media

Content creation and management of social media platforms.

Pastoral Care

Supporting and caring for members of the church community.

Evangelism & Outreach

Reaching the community and spreading the gospel.

Admin Team

Organisation, planning, and operational support.

Children's Church

Teaching and caring for children during services.

Teens / Youth Church

Mentoring and discipling young people.

Welfare Department

Supporting members and providing practical help when needed.

Sanctuary Keepers

Maintaining the church environment and order.

Events & Hospitality

Helping organise church events and welcome guests.

Finance & Procurement

Supporting financial administration and resources.

Transport & Logistics

Helping coordinate movement and setup logistics.
